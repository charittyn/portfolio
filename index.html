Social Media Management

Growing Cyril Gupta’s personal-brand audience

Managed an ongoing content and positioning system across X, LinkedIn and Threads, turning expertise in technology and entrepreneurship into consistent audience-facing content.

View case study details

The objective

Build a stronger and more consistent personal-brand presence, increase visibility and grow an audience around relevant expertise.

What I did

  • Developed content ideas aligned with expertise and personal-brand positioning.
  • Turned knowledge and experiences into posts and threads with stronger hooks and useful insights.
  • Adapted content to each platform instead of simply reposting the same material everywhere.
  • Monitored audience response and used engagement patterns to refine content direction.

Result

The X audience grew from 1,747 followers to 6,385 over the one-year period: 4,638 followers added and 265.5% audience growth.
