Privacy notice

This notice explains the limited personal information handled through this portfolio website.

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Information you choose to send

If you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address and message. That information is used to respond to your enquiry and communicate about the work you asked about.

Contact-form provider

The contact form is submitted through Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service. Information submitted through the form is processed by that service so the enquiry can be delivered.

External links

This website links to external social platforms such as LinkedIn, X, Threads, Facebook and YouTube. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information when you visit them.

Cookies and analytics

This rebuilt version of the portfolio does not include advertising pixels, embedded analytics scripts or the previous LinkedIn badge script. If analytics or other tracking tools are added later, this notice should be updated to describe them.

How long enquiries are kept

Enquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant business records or continue a requested business conversation.

Your questions

For a privacy question or a request relating to information you sent through this site, email hello@charittyn.com.

Note: This is a practical website privacy notice, not legal advice. If the site later adds analytics, advertising pixels, newsletters, accounts or additional data collection, the notice should be reviewed again.
